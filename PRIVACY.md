# LiveLegacy privacy policy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

LiveLegacy does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- The app only talks to the Capture One computer you connect it to, over your own local network. Nothing is sent
  to the developer or to any other server.
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.
- The only things it saves are on your device: the server addresses you type in and the two settings (Prevent
  Sleep and Follow Border). Deleting the app removes them.
- iOS asks for Local Network permission so the app can find Capture One sessions on your network with Bonjour.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/AlexMorrissey-Smith/LiveLegacy/issues.
